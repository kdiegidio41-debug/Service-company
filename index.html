The Everglow Promise

Five steps. You're involved in one of them.

From the first photo of your house to the empty shelf in your garage in January — here's exactly what happens.

  1. Design

    We measure your roofline from satellite, walk the property, and send back a plan showing exactly where every bulb goes — and one flat price for the season.

  2. Build

    Commercial-grade C9 LEDs cut and assembled to your house's exact dimensions in our shop. No gaps, no leftover strand dangling off the corner.

  3. Install

    Clips only — never staples or nails. Cords hidden, timers set for dusk to 11pm, and every socket tested before we pull out of the driveway.

  4. Service

    A strand goes dark in December? Text us. We're back within 24 hours and it costs you nothing. That's the whole point of hiring a company.

  5. Remove & store

    Everything comes down by January 15, gets labeled with your address, and lives on our warehouse shelf until next November. Your garage stays empty.

Questions

The things people ask before booking.

How much does professional Christmas light installation cost?
It depends on the size of your roofline, how many stories you have, and what else you want lit — so we price every house individually rather than off a chart. Send us your address and we'll measure your roofline from satellite imagery and get a real number back to you within 24 hours. Whatever that number is, it's one flat price for the whole season, and it covers the lights, the install, all-season service, takedown, and storage. There is no second invoice in January.
Do I have to buy the lights?
No. We supply commercial-grade C9/C7 LED strands, cut and assembled to your house's exact measurements, and we own and maintain them. That's why year two costs about 30% less — the materials are already made for your house and sitting on our shelf.
Do you really take them down? And when?
Yes, and it's included in every package — never an add-on. Takedown runs January 2 through January 15. We pull everything, including clips and stakes, label your kit with your address, and store it in our warehouse until next November. You don't need a box, a bin, or an inch of garage space.
What happens if a strand or bulb goes out?
Text or call us and we're back within 24 hours, free, any time between install and takedown. That's the 24-Hour Fix and it's the single biggest difference between hiring a company and hiring a guy with a ladder.
Will this damage my roof, gutters, or fascia?
No. We use all-clip attachment — no staples, no nails, no adhesive, ever. We photograph your fascia and gutters before we touch them, and use ladder stabilizers so nothing rests on the gutter. If we mark anything, we put it right.
When should I book?
September and early October. We cap the season's schedule so we can keep the 24-hour service promise, and we're usually full by mid-October. Installs run from late October through December 15, and you'll pick an install week — not a specific day — so weather can't wreck your schedule.
Can you install lights I already own?
Sometimes. If they're commercial-grade and in good shape, yes — and we'll price the labor only. If they're retail strands from a big-box store, we'll usually decline, because we can't stand behind the 24-Hour Fix on materials that weren't built for a full season outdoors. We'll tell you honestly which category yours are in.
Do you do commercial properties?
Yes — retail centers, restaurants, dealerships, office parks, senior living, and HOA entrances. Commercial books in August and September, ahead of residential. Multi-year contracts lock your rate.
How do payments work?
A 50% deposit books your install week, and the balance is due on install day once you've walked the finished job with the crew. Card, ACH, or check. Commercial accounts can go on net-15 terms with a signed agreement.
What if it rains or snows on my install day?
We reschedule. We don't put crews on ladders in rain, ice, or sustained wind over 20mph — which is why we book you an install week rather than a date, and why we hold float days in December. Your lights will be up well before Thanksgiving if that's what you asked for.
