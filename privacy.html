Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This policy explains what information EverGlow Christmas Lighting (“EverGlow,” “we,” “us”) collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. EverGlow Christmas Lighting is a Pennsylvania business based in Jamison, Bucks County.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote or contact us, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address, property address, the services you're interested in, your preferred installation timing, a budget range if you choose to share one, and anything you write in the notes.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, to keep the site secure and working. We don't currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, prepare your design and quote, and schedule and perform the work.
  • To follow up about your quote or service, including reminders about installation and takedown.
  • To keep the business records we're required to keep, such as quotes, agreements, and invoices.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with other companies for their own marketing.

Calls, texts, and email

If you check the consent box on one of our forms, you agree that EverGlow may contact you about your quote and service by phone call, text message, or email at the contact details you gave us, including by automated text messages. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, or by telling us by phone or email. We'll only use your number to contact you about your request and your service unless you separately agree to something else.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our form-processing, email, text-messaging, and scheduling or invoicing tools, and only as needed for them to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests as long as needed to respond and follow up, and customer records as long as needed to provide service and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, unless we're required to keep it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No website or storage system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is meant for adults arranging service for a property. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email or call us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
