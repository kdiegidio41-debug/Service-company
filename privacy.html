Privacy policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Everglow Holiday Lighting Co. ("Everglow", "we", "us") installs and removes holiday lighting in the Philadelphia northern suburbs. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It applies to this website only.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to prepare a quote and get back to you. Through our quote form, that is:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The address of the property you want lit
  • Details about the job — the scope you want, the number of storeys, colour preference, your timing, your budget range, and any notes you add
  • Whether you have had lights installed before, and how you heard about us

We do not ask for and do not want payment card numbers, bank details, Social Security numbers, or any government identification. Never send those through this website.

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and does not run third-party analytics. We do not know who you are until you submit the form or contact us.

How we use it

We use what you give us to prepare your estimate, to contact you about your request, and to carry out the work if you hire us. That is all. Specifically:

  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with data brokers, lead-generation services, or advertisers.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote. If we ever start a mailing list, it will be opt-in and separate.
  • We share information with a service provider only where it is necessary to run the business — for example the form service that delivers your request to our inbox, or an accountant or insurer if you become a customer. They are permitted to use it only to provide that service.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Calls and text messages

When you tick the consent box on the quote form, you are telling us we may call, text or email you about that request. Message and data rates may apply.

You can stop at any time — reply STOP to any text, say so on a call, or email us. We will stop, and it will not affect work already scheduled. We do not send automated marketing texts.

Photographs of your property

We photograph the work we do, including before-and-after shots of your home lit up. If we want to use a photograph of your property publicly — on this site, in an advertisement, or on social media — we ask you first, in the service agreement. You can say no and still be a customer. We never publish your address alongside a photograph.

How long we keep it

If you request a quote and do not hire us, we keep the request for up to two seasons so we recognise you if you come back, then delete it. If you become a customer, we keep job records as long as we need them for warranty, tax and accounting purposes — generally seven years.

Security

This website is a set of static pages served over HTTPS. It holds no database and no customer accounts, so there is nothing stored here to breach. Form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection to our form provider and land in our email.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we ever learn of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you.

Your choices

Email us at hello@everglowlighting.com or call (267) 853-0058 and we will, within a reasonable time and free of charge:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct anything that is wrong, or
  • delete it, unless we are required to keep it for tax or legal reasons.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under state privacy law. Ask us and we will honour them.

Children

This site is meant for adults arranging work on a property. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted on the homepage.
