Estimates and pricing

  • Ranges shown on this site are illustrative, not offers, and we are not bound by them.
  • A binding price is one we put in writing after an on-site assessment, valid for the period stated on it.

What we promise, and where it is written down

We describe our service promises on this site in plain language. Each of them is set out properly in the service agreement, with the conditions that apply:

  • You never climb a ladder. We install, maintain and remove everything.
  • We come back if something fails. Our response window is stated in the agreement and runs from when you tell us, during the lit season, for the lighting we installed.
  • Takedown and storage are included. The takedown window is stated in the agreement. It can be affected by weather and by safe access to the property.

These promises cover the materials and work we supply. They do not cover damage caused by weather events, animals, other contractors, or anyone altering the installation after we leave.

Scheduling, weather and access

Holiday lighting is seasonal and weather-dependent. We schedule in good faith and will keep you informed, but we cannot guarantee a specific date where ice, high wind, snow or unsafe conditions make roof work dangerous. We will not put a crew on a roof in unsafe conditions, and we will reschedule instead.

You are responsible for giving us safe access to the property, and for telling us about anything we should know — a fragile roof, a dog, a security system, a neighbour's easement.

Your responsibilities

  • Confirm you are authorised to have work done at the property. If you rent, or the property is governed by an HOA, get permission first.
  • Give us accurate information. Estimates built on wrong details will change.
  • Do not modify, add to, or remove the installation yourself during the season. If something is wrong, call us — that is what the service promise is for.
