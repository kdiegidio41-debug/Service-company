# Topic backlog

Work top to bottom. When a video is finished, move its line to `log.md`. Mix small home topics
with a few big ones, and favor topics people actually search ("how is a ___ built").

Rules for picking a topic:
- It has to be buildable as a chain of at least 8 "but… so you…" problems.
- Don't copy another channel's topics in the same order, especially @youbuiltit's (wells, water
  towers, running water). Do those later, if at all, from a different angle.
- Every fact has to hold up. If you can't verify a claim, cut it.
