# Product shortlist, 18 November 2026

Two `product-scout` runs (everyday problem-solvers, and hobby gear), about 80 searches,
then spot checks on the top picks. **No candidate passed every filter on verified
evidence.** The demand is real in each case below; the supplier cost is not verified for
any of them, and that is the number that decides whether a product works with paid ads.

Next step for all three: a real landed-cost quote (product plus US shipping) from
CJdropshipping, Zendrop or AliExpress, then a sample order. The research container
could not reach those sites, Reddit, or Exploding Topics.

---

## 1. Hands-free needlepoint stand for stretcher bars (best market)

An adjustable lap or table stand whose clamp fits needlepoint stretcher bars, so the
stitcher has both hands free and can flip the canvas to check the back.

**Why it has potential**
- Needlepoint is the fastest-growing hobby the scouts found:
  - Gen Z searches for needlepoint bags were up 550% year over year ([Axios, Apr 2026](https://www.axios.com/local/austin/2026/04/10/austin-needlepoint-popularity-grows)).
  - NYC shops report beginner kits selling out within days ([YPulse, Jan 2026](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2026/01/20/needlepoint-is-the-latest-granny-hobby-gen-z-cant-put-down/)).
- A clear price gap:
  - Specialty stands sold through needlepoint shops are expensive. The System 4 is $60-195 for the stand ([Penny Linn](https://pennylinn.com/products/needlework-system-4-lap-table-stand-with-radius-arm)), and its stretcher-bar clamp is sold separately ([Stitching Fox](https://www.stitchingfox.com/collections/system-4-stands/products/lap-table-stand-stitching-fox)).
  - Cheap Amazon and Walmart stands ($22-49) are built for embroidery hoops, and retailers warn their clamps don't fit some frames.
  - Nobody sells a modern, affordable stand aimed at the new needlepoint crowd.
- Indoor winter hobby: a January launch lands in its peak season.

**Economics (estimates, not verified)**
- Landed cost: about $12-18.
- Price: $59-69.
- At $69 with an $18 landed cost: about $48 gross profit, a break-even CPA of about $48, and a break-even ROAS of about 1.4.

**What could kill it**
- **Hard to advertise.** There's no big "wow" moment, so this one needs organic
  stitch-with-me content more than cold paid ads.
- **Clamp quality and fit.** A clamp that doesn't grip stretcher bars means returns. The
  sample must be tested with real bars.
- **Copying the System 4 or Lowery design.** Their patents are unverified; source a
  different design.
- **Box size and weight:** medium.

## 2. Electric yarn winder + swift "hank to cake" set (best video)

A cordless winder plus a yarn swift, sold as a set: it turns a twisted hank of yarn into a
center-pull cake in about a minute.

**Why it has potential**
- Crochet and knitting demand is verified:
  - Searches for analog crafts were up 136% in six months, and yarn accessory sales were up 40% year over year (Michaels 2026 Creativity Trend Report, reported by [AOL](https://www.aol.com/lifestyle/8-biggest-crafting-trends-2026-194606392.html)).
  - The top Amazon winders sell about 2,000 units a month ([ASINSIGHT](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/caydo-electric-yarn-winder), a third-party estimate).
- The yarn cake winding up is a naturally satisfying video.
- Same buyer as #1, which opens the door to a fiber-arts accessories brand rather than
  a one-product store.

**Economics (estimates, not verified)**
- Landed cost: about $25-32 for the set.
- Price: $69-79.
- At $74 with a $30 landed cost: about $41 gross profit, and a break-even ROAS of about 1.8.

**What could kill it**
- **It's already crowded.** Caydo runs its own store ([caydo.com](https://www.caydo.com/collections/electric-yarn-winder)), Amazon generics sell for $27-45, and the cordless Etcokei is $59.99. The set has to look clearly better than a $27 winder.
- **Quality.** Reviews of generics mention squeaking, units dying after three months, and
  weak tension arms.
- **Shipping the battery.** The lithium battery in cordless units complicates air shipping.

## 3. Premium gold panning starter kit (best hook, wrong season)

A curated kit: two pans with riffles, a classifier, a snuffer bottle, vials, tweezers, a
magnifier, and a backpack. Positioned as a modern "weekend prospector" kit.

**Why it has potential**
- Gold passed $4,500 an ounce, with news coverage of Americans taking up panning
  ([IBTimes, about May 2026](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/modern-gold-rush-americans-prospecting-1806009)).
- The best three-second hook of anything found: the sand washes out and gold flakes
  glint in the pan.
- The Amazon price zone is $20-50, and there's room above it for a premium kit
  ([ASINSIGHT](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/gold-panning-kit)).

**Economics (estimates, not verified)**
- Landed cost: about $15-25 (bulky box).
- Price: $59.
- About $32-42 gross profit.

**What could kill it**
- **The season.** River panning is a summer activity. Launch it in April, not now.
- **The gold price.** Demand depends on it, and a pullback cools interest.
- **Established brands.** Garrett, Minelab and SE hold Amazon, and China-shipped kits are
  already on Fruugo ([example](https://www.fruugo.us/gold-panning-kit-15-inch-pan-for-efficient-gold-particle-capture-and-river-prospecting/p-458767764)).
- **Ad policy.** No "make money" or income claims in ads.
