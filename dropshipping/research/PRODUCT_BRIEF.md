# Product brief: Denly Den Table

A dog crate that looks like furniture (an end table or nightstand). Two sizes
(Medium, Large) and three finishes (walnut, black, white). Store config:
`store/products/denly-den-table/product.json`.

**Status: not validated.** Demand is evidenced; supplier cost, quality and real
delivery time are not. Nothing launches until the sample checklist below is done.

## The promise

> The dog crate that looks like furniture, so you can stop hiding it.

The store headline, the ads and the product title all make this one promise.

## Buyer and trigger

- Dog owners, mostly 25-45 and mostly women, in homes or apartments they care about
  decorating.
- They're triggered by:
  - a new puppy or a rescue
  - moving to a smaller or nicer place
  - being tired of a wire crate with a blanket thrown over it

## Claims

**Allowed:** "looks like furniture," the finishes, the tabletop, sizes and dimensions from
the spec sheet, free shipping from a US warehouse, 30-day returns, and the delivery window.

**Not allowed:**
- "Solid wood," unless it is.
- "Chew-proof" or "escape-proof."
- Anything about anxiety, calming or behavior.
- "Vet approved."
- Invented reviews.
- A fake "compare at" price.

## Sample checklist

Order one in the size and finish you'll photograph first, then check:

1. **Delivery:** how many days did it really take, and what shape was the box in?
2. **Materials:** what is it made of? Do the finish and hardware look better than the $75 Amazon
   ones?
3. **Assembly:** time it. Does anything wobble once it's built?
4. **Weight limit:** confirm the tabletop limit and the inside and outside dimensions.
5. **Latch:** do the door and latch close firmly?
6. **Footage:** film everything in the store's shot list while you have it. That footage is the
   first round of ads.

## Launch plan for a budget under $300

1. **Sample.** About $80-150 including shipping. That's unverified until you have a quote.
2. **Free videos.** Post 5-10 short ones on TikTok and Reels. The before and after (wire crate,
   then Den Table) is the main hook.
3. **Go or no-go.** Connect checkout and spend the rest on ads only if videos get real response.
   That means comments asking where to buy, or saves and shares well above your usual. A
   $100-150 ad budget is a signal check, not a full test.
