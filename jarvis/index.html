              What you can say

              Say “hey Jarvis” and then the command, or hold nothing and just press space to skip the wake word. No mic? Type any of these in the bar at the bottom — every command works both ways.

              The briefing

              • Brief me — everything, in order
              • How's the app doing
              • What's the revenue
              • How are downloads
              • How did content do
              • How's TikTok doing
              • What was the top post

              Autonomy

              • What did you handle
              • What needs me
              • Refresh the numbers

              Content ops

              • Queue a post about …
              • Schedule a TikTok about … tomorrow
              • What's in the queue
              • Mark … as posted
              • Give me hooks about …
              • Write a script about …

              Memory

              • Remember …
              • Remind me to …
              • What are my ideas

              Keys

              • space — talk without the wake word
              • / — focus the command line
              • B — briefing
              • M — mic on / off
              • esc — shut it up
              • ? — this panel
